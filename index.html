Mouse: drag to orbit, right-drag or Shift-drag to pan, wheel to zoom, double-click a part to focus it. Touch: one finger orbits, two fingers pan and pinch-zoom, double-tap focuses.

Model: procedurally sculpted for this project from signed-distance primitives (no third-party meshes). Rendering by three.js r128 (MIT). Educational illustration, not a medical reference.
